Credit approval classifier

Personal project · 2026

A reproducible pipeline comparing six models for credit approval classification.

Details: Credit approval classifier

I built a reproducible machine learning pipeline to compare six approaches to credit approval classification, from logistic regression to a stacking ensemble.

  • Applied SMOTEENN within each cross-validation fold to prevent resampling leakage.
  • Tracked experiments with MLflow and tuned models with Optuna.
  • The best model achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.91 and a Gini coefficient of 0.82 in the project evaluation.

Models, evaluation, and interpretability

I compared logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, and a stacking ensemble using 10-fold cross-validation. Evaluation included KS, PR-AUC, and expected calibration error, alongside AUC-ROC and Gini.

I used SHAP for model explanations and included a disparate-impact analysis.
